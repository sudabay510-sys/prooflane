# ProofLane — Brand identity

**Slug:** `prooflane` · **Tagline:** The inquiry-to-delivery workflow plan for independent photographers.
**Status:** Live. Payments processed by Gumroad (Monthly or Quarterly).

## Positioning
Wedding, portrait, brand, and product photographers who aren’t ready for a full CRM — or want a clear plan on top of one.

**Promise (honest):** Workflow education only. No booking-volume or income guarantees.

## Personas
- **Newer pro:** Booking a few shoots a month and making up the process each time.
- **Busy season shooter:** Great clients, chaotic inbox, late galleries.
- **CRM owner:** You have the tool — you need the order of operations.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Inquiry** — pain: Replies go out days late. → plan: A reply template and two daily inbox windows.
2. **Booking** — pain: Leads go quiet after the quote. → plan: A booking guide and day-2/day-7 follow-ups.
3. **Shoot prep** — pain: Last-minute timeline scrambles. → plan: Questionnaire + shot list + 72-hour confirm.
4. **Delivery** — pain: Galleries slip and reviews never come. → plan: Sneak-peek rule, delivery checklist, honest review ask.

## Voice & tone
Quietly confident and editorial, like a seasoned second shooter who keeps everyone on time.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing
- **Monthly** — $29 / month. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.
- **Quarterly** — $69 every 3 months. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.

## Do
- Use photographer vocabulary (cull, sneak peek, gallery).
- Keep the dark ‘darkroom’ look with amber highlights.
- Show Monthly and Quarterly prices, plus the renewal sentence, before checkout.
- Save the customer’s email before sending them to Gumroad.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Imply affiliation with HoneyBook, Pixieset, or any CRM.
- Promise more weddings, bookings, or income.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
